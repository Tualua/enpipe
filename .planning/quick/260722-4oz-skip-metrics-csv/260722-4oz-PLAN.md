<objective>
When metrics are not computed (`--no-metrics`), do not create the `<out>.metrics.csv`
artifact and do not emit the metrics/ИТОГО summary logs.

Purpose: The CSV is a *metrics* artifact. Under `--no-metrics`, `rows` is still
populated with size/time data plus `None` for every SSIM/PSNR field, so today an
empty-of-quality CSV is written. Gate the emission on `metrics_on` so "no metrics
computed → no artifact".

Output: One-line guard change in `pipeline.py` plus test coverage proving the
gate in both directions. `write_metrics_csv` in `metrics.py` stays untouched (pure).
</objective>
